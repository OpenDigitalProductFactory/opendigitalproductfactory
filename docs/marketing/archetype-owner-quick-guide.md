## How To Read This

DPF's archetype story starts with the owner-practitioner: the person who is still doing the work customers pay for while also carrying scheduling, follow-up, quoting, stock, compliance, marketing, finance, and operational coordination.

The core promise across the catalog is:

> Keep doing the work customers pay you for. Give the work around the work to governed AI coworkers.

This guide is category-first because that is how customers, marketers, and testers usually need to think. The leaf archetypes are listed so the current source coverage stays visible.

## How This Feeds Assets

Use this guide as the first cut when deciding what to make:

- **Still image:** show the owner in the real setting with DPF visible as a calm work surface.
- **Diagram:** put the core paid work in the center and surround it with the necessary-evil jobs an AI coworker can prepare.
- **Short video:** open with pressure, show the coworker scan, let the owner approve the sensitive action, then return the owner to customers, crew, patients, members, residents, guests, or clients.
- **Test case:** verify the fields, states, language, refusal, or approval path that makes the marketing claim true.

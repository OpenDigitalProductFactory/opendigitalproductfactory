A new finite delivery room retains its initial outcome and acceptance criteria.
Open **Delivery details** beside the outcome to see the agreement, linked
Customer 0 source and check time, and the completion handoff.

A trusted link alone does not mean guidance was read. A scoped mirrored source
is a dated snapshot; unavailable guidance names what the delivery owner must
check. Local requirements and permissions still govern the work.

The normal completion checks decide whether delivery is proven. A closed room
with no handoff says **Completion handoff missing**. Verified delivery keeps its
evidence and separately linked follow-on work. Those new items do not replace
the agreed outcome. Changed criterion statements require explicit reconciliation;
harmless source line changes do not add work. Historical rooms receive no invented
agreement or completion history.

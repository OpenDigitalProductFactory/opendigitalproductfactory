## Definition

The **Scrum Master** is accountable for the **team's effectiveness** and for **establishing Scrum** as defined in the Scrum Guide. It is a leadership-by-service role, discharged across three audiences.

## Three Services

- **Serves the team** — coaches self-management and cross-functionality, and **removes impediments** to progress.
- **Serves the Product Owner** — techniques for goal definition, Product Backlog management, and empirical planning.
- **Serves the organization** — leads Scrum adoption, coaches the org, and removes barriers between stakeholders and teams.

## Heuristic

Forecast delivery **empirically from throughput** — the number of items finished per unit of time — rather than from a promised number. Forecasting is a decision input, not a commitment.

## How To Forecast

- **Throughput** gives an empirical basis for projecting delivery; reviewing historical throughput helps the team understand capacity and discuss likely dates.
- A **cumulative flow diagram** visualizes WIP, average cycle time, and average throughput together — the health view behind the forecast.
- Forecasting stays **empirical, not a commitment**, consistent with inspect-and-adapt; SAFe's "take an economic view" frames forecasts as inputs to decisions.

> Note: the canonical open source (Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams, CC-BY-SA) uses **throughput**, not story-point velocity; the 2020 Scrum Guide does not define "velocity." This page is therefore grounded in throughput-based forecasting.

## How DPF Coworkers Use It

- Forecast from rolling throughput; present ranges, not single dates.
- Depends on healthy flow — see [[professions/scrum-master/wip-limits-and-flow]].

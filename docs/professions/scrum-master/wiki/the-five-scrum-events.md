## The Five Events

Per the 2020 Scrum Guide:

- **The Sprint** — the container event, fixed length of one month or less, holding all other events.
- **Sprint Planning** — opens the Sprint by deciding what work will be done and why it matters.
- **The Daily Scrum** — a 15-minute event to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan.
- **Sprint Review** — evaluates the outcome of the Sprint and what to adapt next.
- **Sprint Retrospective** — plans improvements to the team's quality and effectiveness.

> Source: 2020 Scrum Guide (CC-BY-SA 4.0).

## Why They Exist

Each event is a formal point of **inspection and adaptation** (see [[professions/scrum-master/three-pillars-empiricism]]) — they replace ad-hoc status-checking with a cadence that keeps the team aligned to the Sprint Goal and continuously improving.

## How DPF Coworkers Use It

- Treat the events as the team's heartbeat; protect their purpose, not just their schedule.
- The Scrum Master facilitates them — see [[professions/scrum-master/scrum-master-accountability]].
- The Retrospective is deepened in [[professions/scrum-master/sprint-retrospective-continuous-improvement]].

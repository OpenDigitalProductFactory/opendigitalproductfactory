## Heuristic

Run the **Sprint Retrospective** as the team's planned mechanism to **improve its quality and effectiveness**. It applies the inspection + adaptation pillars to the team's **own process**, not just the product.

## Make Improvement Stick

- **Inspect the process**, not only the increment — what helped, what hindered, what to change.
- **Feedback loops** are an explicit Kanban practice driving ongoing improvement.
- **Track actions to closure** — treat each improvement as an inspectable work item the team owns, not a wish that evaporates by the next Sprint.

> Sources: 2020 Scrum Guide (CC-BY-SA); Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams (CC-BY-SA).

## Why

A retrospective that produces no tracked change is the failure mode the pillars warn against: inspection without adaptation. Converting findings into owned, closed actions is what makes "continuous improvement" real rather than ritual.

## How DPF Coworkers Use It

- Facilitate the retro to surface honest signal (psychological safety), then convert findings into tracked actions.
- This is the team-process application of [[professions/scrum-master/three-pillars-empiricism]].

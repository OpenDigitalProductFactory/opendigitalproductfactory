## Heuristic

Dry-cleaning and laundry work is a custody workflow before it is a cleaning workflow. The customer gives the business garments or household textiles; the business issues a claim ticket, tags the items, routes them through a counter, plant, workroom, or delivery route, and returns the same property against a promised ready date.

For a fabric-care operator:

- Issue one claim ticket per customer order and keep every garment, bag, or alteration line tied to it.
- Capture care instructions, stains, existing damage, special handling, and event deadlines at intake, before the work disappears into the plant.
- Update the customer when the order is ready, and update earlier when the ready promise is at risk.
- Treat missing, mixed, damaged, or unclear items as custody exceptions that need owner or manager attention.

## Why it matters

The customer's trust is in the return, not only the cleaning result. A shirt cleaned perfectly but returned to the wrong customer, a wedding dress delayed past the event, or a claim ticket with no item trail is an operational failure. The tag and ticket chain is the lightweight control that lets satellite stores, the central plant, and delivery routes act as one business.

## Heuristic

Keep enterprise views for livestock, hay/forage, crops, grazing, breeding, and custom services while reconciling them to the operation’s financial system. Distinguish owned inventory, raised inventory, purchased feed or inputs, work in progress, capital equipment, repairs, land costs, and outside services.

The seasonal cash view should show committed costs before production, expected ranges rather than one fabricated yield or sale price, debt and insurance dates, taxes and verified exemptions, and the point where a delayed sale or failed crop changes liquidity.

For a sell-or-hold decision, compare dated, like-for-like market evidence with the operation’s actual class, weight, grade, quality, location, freight, shrink, commissions, checkoffs, feed or storage cost, welfare constraints, capacity, and cash need. Show scenarios and sensitivity. The finance coworker may prepare the brief but does not trade, broker, promise a price, or choose tax treatment.

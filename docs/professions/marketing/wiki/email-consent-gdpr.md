## Jurisdiction & Competency

**Jurisdiction:** EU/EEA. The US counterpart is the opt-out [[professions/marketing/email-consent-can-spam]]. **Competency:** practitioner.

## The EU Model Is Opt-In

Under GDPR Article 7, the controller must be able to **demonstrate** that the data subject consented **before** processing. Marketing email to EU recipients requires prior, provable opt-in.

Conditions for valid consent:

- **Demonstrable** — the controller must be able to show consent was given.
- **Clearly distinguishable** — the consent request is separate, intelligible, and in plain language; no bundling or pre-ticked boxes.
- **Freely given** — not conditioned on unnecessary processing.
- **Withdrawable** — "as easy to withdraw as to give consent," at any time.

## Contrast With CAN-SPAM

GDPR opt-in is the **structural opposite** of US CAN-SPAM's send-until-opt-out. A flow built only for CAN-SPAM is unlawful for EU recipients. When a recipient could fall under both, apply the stricter rule (GDPR opt-in). This is also an expression of [[professions/marketing/marketing-ethics]] (honesty, respect for the audience).

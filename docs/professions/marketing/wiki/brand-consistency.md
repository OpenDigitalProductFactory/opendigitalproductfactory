## Heuristic

Keep the offering and its communications **coherent across all four Ps** — a promise made in **Promotion** must be delivered by the **Product**, the **Price**, and the **Place**. Inconsistency between what marketing says and what the customer experiences erodes trust faster than any single campaign builds it.

## Why It Is Expert Work

Consistency spans functions and channels, so it is a cross-cutting discipline:

- **Across the mix** — claims in promotion must hold in the actual product/price/availability ([[professions/marketing/marketing-mix-4ps]]).
- **An ethics expression** — honesty and transparency mean no deceptive subject lines or claims the experience does not deliver ([[professions/marketing/marketing-ethics]]).
- **Consistent consent posture** — a brand that respects audiences applies the **stricter** consent rule when jurisdictions mix, rather than the most permissive (GDPR opt-in over CAN-SPAM opt-out when both apply).

Consistency exists to preserve the value-for-customers-and-society that defines marketing.

## How DPF Coworkers Use It

- Audit campaigns for promise-vs-delivery gaps across the 4 Ps.
- Default to the stricter consent regime in mixed-jurisdiction audiences.

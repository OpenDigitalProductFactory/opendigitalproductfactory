## Heuristic

Build offers and outreach from what the operation can truthfully deliver: product or animal class, grade or quality evidence where applicable, quantity range, location, availability window, handling or delivery terms, buyer fit, and verified production or stewardship claims. Do not turn an aspiration, forecast, exemption, treatment record, certification-in-progress, or generic rural image into a public claim.

Separate three evidence layers:

1. **Operation facts** — current inventory, field or herd status, capacity, sale readiness, prior outcomes, and approved proof assets.
2. **Published market observations** — dated USDA AMS or other authoritative reports matched to class, quality, unit, geography, and time period.
3. **Marketing judgment** — target buyer, channel, offer framing, timing, and draft message, with uncertainty stated.

The marketing coworker may prepare a farm-direct offer, buyer brief, sale-barn comparison, breeding-stock proof outline, hay availability notice, or seasonal content plan. The owner approves claims, price, timing, audience, and send/publish action. Market evidence does not authorize a sale, and marketing copy does not establish that a pesticide, animal-health, organic, conservation, tax, or regulatory claim is lawful.

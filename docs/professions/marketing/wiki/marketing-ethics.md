## Rule

Marketing conduct is governed by three ethical norms: **do no harm**, **foster trust and integrity** across the ecosystem, and **embrace ethical values**. These bound every campaign and message.

## Core Values

The AMA Statement of Ethics names values that operationalize the norms:

- **Honesty** — no manipulation, coercion, or deception.
- **Responsibility** — protect sensitive information; own the consequences of marketing actions.
- **Equity** — respect vulnerable segments; reject harmful stereotyping.
- **Transparency** — disclose conflicts and foreseeable risks.
- **Citizenship** — act for the broader good.

## What It Is

The **marketing mix** — the "4 Ps" — is the set of controllable variables used to influence and meet target-customer needs:

- **Product** — the offering itself.
- **Price** — what customers pay.
- **Place** — distribution and availability.
- **Promotion** — marketing communications.

It was coined by **E. Jerome McCarthy (1960)** in *Basic Marketing*, building on earlier work, and later popularized widely.

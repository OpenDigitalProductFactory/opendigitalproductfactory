## Weather evidence

Keep current observations, short-range operational forecasts, extended outlooks, and seasonal climate outlooks distinct. Record source, issue time, valid period, geography, variable, and confidence. The farther the horizon, the more the output should describe scenarios and preparatory choices rather than a precise work date.

## Market evidence

Compare like with like: species or commodity, class, weight or grade, quality, location, sale type, freight basis, volume, and report date. Preserve the report and filters behind a comparison. A regional average or old auction result is context, not the price this operation will receive.

## Decision brief

For a sell-now, hold, contract, buy-feed, cut-hay, or service-booking question, show:

- the operator’s objective and latest decision date;
- current inventory, condition, cash, feed, capacity, and welfare constraints;
- dated weather and market evidence;
- at least two scenarios and the triggers that move between them;
- downside, reversibility, and authority required;
- the next observation or official report that should refresh the brief.

The coworker does not place a sale, promise a price, or contact a market without explicit approval.

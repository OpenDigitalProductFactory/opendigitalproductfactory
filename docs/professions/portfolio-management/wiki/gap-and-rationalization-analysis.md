## Heuristic

Run two complementary analyses on the portfolio:

- **Gap analysis** — compare the current investment mix against strategic objectives to expose **under- and over-funded** areas.
- **Rationalization** — "eliminate projects lacking business alignment so they don't waste resources."

Do both **recurringly**, not once: portfolios accrete low-value and misaligned work over time, and pruning protects the portfolio's overall value.

## Why It Is Expert Work

Cutting funded work is politically and analytically hard — it requires judging alignment against strategy, comparing across heterogeneous initiatives, and absorbing the organizational friction of stopping things. Done well, freed capacity is **redirected toward higher-cost-of-delay work** via [[professions/portfolio-management/wsjf-prioritization]] re-sequencing.

## How To Apply

1. **Map spend to objectives** — find where strategy is starved and where it is over-served.
2. **Identify misaligned work** — anything not contributing to strategy is a rationalization candidate.
3. **Cut and redirect** — stop low-value work; re-sequence the freed capacity by WSJF.
4. This is how [[professions/portfolio-management/balance-the-portfolio]] is maintained over time.

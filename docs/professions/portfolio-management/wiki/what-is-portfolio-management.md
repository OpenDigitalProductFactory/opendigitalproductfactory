## Definition

**Portfolio management** is "the process of selecting, prioritizing, and administering" an organization's portfolio of investments to achieve business objectives. Its defining job is to do the **right things** — directing limited resources to the work most significant to strategic goals.

It spans a **lifecycle** — select → prioritize → resource → monitor → rationalize — not one-time project picking.

At scale this is reframed as **Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)**, which "aligns strategy and execution" across strategy/investment funding, portfolio operations, and governance.

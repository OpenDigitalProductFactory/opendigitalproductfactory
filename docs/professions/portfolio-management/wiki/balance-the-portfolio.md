## Rule

**Balance** the portfolio: reconcile **demand against available capacity** to prevent overallocation and bottlenecks, and fund the **highest-value opportunities** while reserving capacity — rather than loading every initiative at once.

## Why

A portfolio that starts everything finishes nothing: overallocation creates bottlenecks and stalls flow across all initiatives. A healthy mix funds the most valuable work and holds capacity in reserve. In **Lean budgeting**, this balance is struck at the **value-stream level** — investment goes to the value streams that matter most, not spread evenly.

## How To Apply

1. **Cap demand to capacity** — do not commit beyond what the organization can deliver.
2. **Fund by value** — concentrate investment on the highest-value opportunities; reserve slack.
3. **Order with WSJF** — [[professions/portfolio-management/wsjf-prioritization]] supplies the economic ordering signal.
4. **Prune to rebalance** — see [[professions/portfolio-management/gap-and-rationalization-analysis]].

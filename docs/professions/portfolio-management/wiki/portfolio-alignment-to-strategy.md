## Rule

**Every funded item must contribute to organizational goals.** Portfolio management exists to ensure "every project … contributes to its goals and strategy" — alignment is not a nice-to-have, it is the admission criterion.

## How Alignment Is Enforced

- **In prioritization** — criteria explicitly weigh strategic alignment alongside business impact, urgency, and risk; alignment is a first-class scoring dimension.
- **At the portfolio level** — Lean Portfolio Management exists to "align strategy and execution," connecting investment funding to strategic intent.
- **As a cut trigger** — misalignment is a rationalization signal: work that does not serve strategy is a candidate to stop ([[professions/portfolio-management/gap-and-rationalization-analysis]]).

## How To Apply

1. **Require a strategy link** for every funded item; reject orphans.
2. **Score alignment** in prioritization, not just value/urgency.
3. **Re-test alignment recurringly** — strategy shifts; the portfolio must follow.

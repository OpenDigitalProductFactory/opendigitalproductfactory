## Why it's cited

The anchor for `[[principles/findability-is-part-of-capture]]`. Morville's "found-or-it-doesn't-exist" framing is exactly the test a knowledge platform must apply at capture time: a fact written somewhere the agent can't route to again was not captured, only misplaced. DPF makes findability a property of the act of storing, not a hope checked after a failed search.

## Key claims

- Information that cannot be located is functionally absent — storage without findability is loss.
- Findability is a design property of the store (addressing, labels, metadata, navigation), not a post-hoc add-on.
- The retrieval round-trip — can it be found again? — is the real test of whether something was captured.

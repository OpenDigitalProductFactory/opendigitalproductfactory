## Compatibility, risk and rollback

There is no schema migration. Old binaries reject the ED policy. Deploy compatible
readers/writers before admitting it, retain accepted relations and fences, and
never roll back to a binary that cannot exclude late writes or preserve accepted
allocation history. Rollback disables new admission while compatible readers and
writers remain; it cannot refund or remove an accepted assessment.

The main risk is widening authority by confusing operator direction, independent
policy approval and execution settlement. Separate immutable evidence and exact
source matching prevent that confusion. Missing historical effects stay an
incident even if the prospective restricted assessment passes. Missing funding
ownership remains unresolved; no allocation is inferred from the install.

# TAK-JSI qualification issuance, revalidation, and activation binding

Status: proposed canonical design for BI-6DB95601. Not an approval, migration, or pilot result. Extends the [PAAW competence-evolution design §7–9](2026-08-30-paaw-competence-evolution-workroom-design.md#7-promotion-to-qualification-interlock) and supplies the qualification producer boundary for BI-687E7D81.

## Outcomes and acceptance

**OBJ-ISSUE:** Issue one canonical, verifiable qualification version for the exact GAID, job, activity, operating composition and context after an authorized, evidence-backed assessment.

**OBJ-REVALIDATE:** Decide and persist the impact on every in-scope live qualification before a promoted operating change can execute. Require target-profile assessment wherever retained evidence no longer supports the proposed use.

**OBJ-ACTIVATE:** Make TAK activation and rollback depend on fresh, compatible qualification and the independent authority ceiling. Project the reason and next accountable action to the people operating Living Playbooks.

**OBJ-CONSUME:** Let S5 consume a typed decision now without pretending a missing scheme or unreadable record is proof. Later show different server-enforced JSI ceilings for two real coworkers on the same session launch path.

| Acceptance ID | Objective IDs | Statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC-ISSUE | OBJ-ISSUE | Only a declared, currently authorized issuer can issue from accepted, immutable assessment evidence for the exact resolved GAID, scheme version, job-profile version, operating-profile fingerprint and scope. Test a valid issuance, changed inputs, unknown evidence, replay and self-issuance refusal. |
| AC-IMPACT | OBJ-REVALIDATE | A material promotion cannot leave an affected active qualification silently valid. Every candidate has one attributable impact decision for the exact change and qualification version. Retention requires explicit no-impact and unchanged verified scope. |
| AC-SERIAL | OBJ-REVALIDATE, OBJ-ACTIVATE | Promotion, revalidation and activation contend on the same persisted transition boundary. Missing, superseded or conflicting impact evidence prevents consequential activation, including under concurrent issuance or asset change. |
| AC-TAK | OBJ-ACTIVATE | TAK reads only the intersection of an active compatible qualification, explicit current binding, promotion scope, grants, data/regulatory policy and live operational evidence. Non-active records never supply positive authority. |
| AC-ROLLBACK | OBJ-ACTIVATE | Restoring a prior method or profile does not restore stale qualification. Rollback checks current profile, scheme, evidence, subject, scope, binding and freshness or stops the affected activity. |
| AC-OPERATOR | OBJ-REVALIDATE, OBJ-ACTIVATE | Existing Living Playbooks shows unaffected, pending-revalidation, restricted, suspended, active and rejected outcomes distinctly, each with a next accountable action where one is owed. |
| AC-S5 | OBJ-CONSUME | No scheme resolves not-applicable only when absence is established. Required-but-absent and unreadable data cannot qualify. With two genuinely issued records and otherwise eligible sessions in the same Workroom stage, launch and invocation show differing JSI ceilings and reject cross-scope calls without client hooks. |
| AC-CONSOLIDATE | OBJ-REVALIDATE, OBJ-ACTIVATE | At least 20% of planned delivery effort consolidates material-change resolution and qualification/binding transitions. The plan identifies eliminated checks and projections, not just new files. |

Non-goals include a second decision ledger or permission engine, a private S5 qualification table, a new GAID identifier or modification to `getAgentGaidMap`, model-weight training, automatic wider authority from outcomes, federation of qualification, and claims of certification or accreditation.

## Research & Benchmarking

The design follows the DPF TAK-JSI standard. [ISO/IEC 17024:2026](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso-iec%3A17024%3Aed-3%3Av1%3Aen) is an informative analogue for scheme-bound assessment, impartial decisions and periodic reassessment. It applies to certification of persons. DPF does not adopt it as normative for agents or claim conformity.

| Comparator | Adopt | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open source · [SPIRE workload registration](https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/deploying/registering/) and [short-lived SVIDs](https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/deploying/svids/) | Trust server-resolved subject properties and explicit validity instead of a caller's asserted identity. | Do not issue a second identity or treat workload identity attestation as proof of job competence. |
| Open source · [Sigstore Policy Controller](https://docs.sigstore.dev/policy-controller/overview/) | Verify exact artifact identity and applicable evidence at admission, with independently applicable constraints all enforced. | A signed assertion is not an execution grant. Use current evidence and scope, not a tag, portable bearer claim or external policy engine. |
| Commercial · [Microsoft Entra entitlement management](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/id-governance/entitlement-management-overview) | Separate policy, approval and expiring assignment. Re-evaluate rather than treating assignment as permanent. | Do not bundle qualification into a resource grant or equate group membership with competence. |
| Commercial · [AWS Signer revocation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/signer/latest/developerguide/revocation.html) | Distinguish an immutable issuance fact from current admissibility. Check current revocation at use. | Do not infer job fitness from signatures, or assume re-deploying an older artifact makes its approval current again. |

A grant-only design conflates fitness with authority. A receipt-only design either scans history on the TAK path or quietly builds a second mutable truth in S5. A parallel qualification service adds a second identity and enforcement path. Use one DPF-owned qualification relation, canonical evidence references and the live TAK intersection.

## Issuer and data contract

**Scheme applicability belongs to the governed job/activity policy.** A scheme is a versioned declaration of criteria and evidence requirements, issuer authority, critical failures, validity and material-change policy. Resolve its exact revision through the existing job/profile and policy sources. An explicit absent-scheme result is recorded only after a successful authoritative lookup or when an activation is explicitly in the pre-scheme rollout cohort. A job/activity policy that requires qualification but cannot resolve its scheme returns required-unavailable. Do not infer applicability from ORM delegate presence, a missing row, a failed query or a user-supplied flag. The pilot must obtain a real approved scheme for each selected activity. Do not seed passing records or designate a demo score as qualification.

**The server resolves the subject.** Use the installation organization and admitted current Principal-to-GAID link, authoritative job/activity assignment, exact job/scheme versions, scope (workflow, archetype, jurisdiction, data/risk class), and one versioned operating-profile manifest using canonical JSON. Resolve model/provider/routing, prompts, directives, skills, corpora, memory/retrieval policy, enabled tools/connectors, authority inputs, decision criteria/weights, data policy and runtime/harness revisions. Store versioned references/digests, not raw content or credentials. Fetch these from the actual candidate or active execution profile rather than input claims. If a component cannot be resolved or is mutable without a revision/fence, the profile is not yet issuable. Identity and profile continuity are independent. Preserve the existing AIDOC/GAID wire format until its versioned projection can distinguish a complete manifest from legacy material.

**Assessment is not issuance.** The assessment workflow resolves an immutable accepted result that binds the exact subject and profile above to a predeclared scheme and workload. Use the governing Workroom/TaskRun for execution provenance and existing evidence and receipt carriers for frozen criteria, representative held-out work, scope, evidence digests, outcome, critical-failure verdict and responsible assessor. The result must carry `pass`, `fail` or `inconclusive`. The shared evidence reader verifies integrity, source authority and freshness. Pass allows the declared issuer to consider issuance, not to self-approve a profile. Issuer authority is checked at effect time, and assessor independence follows scheme rules with the subject never acting as issuer or verifier of its own result. The low-risk pilot uses a named human scheme authority and a separate accepted assessor result through an existing governed human gate. A missing capability or writer is a hold, not permission to write through Prisma, trust a caller-supplied assessor/decision ID, or fall back to self-assessment. Binding or activation remains a separate governed action.

**One proposed `JsiQualification` carrier.** Use an immutable issued version with a stable record identifier, versioned lineage, standard/scheme/job versions, GAID and canonical local Principal relation, scope and exact operating-profile fingerprint, allowed operation keys and exclusions, data/risk/oversight ceilings, validity window, issuer/assessor references and assurance level, assessment re-verification reference, and material-change/surveillance policy references. Current status is a closed JSI state, distinct from structural `RecordLifecycle`. Replace rather than edit the assertion of fitness. Use an indexed successor relation and status revision for compare-and-set transitions. Reject invalid windows and ambiguous current coverage on the write and read sides. On newly created data, enforce a unique issued version and at most one current active version per exact subject/scheme/job/activity/context slot. The resolver admits a covering record only when coverage is unambiguous. It never unions two records to reach a wider ceiling.

Use one normalized profile-dependency association to index stable input references and revisions for impact fan-out. It records relationships to the qualification version, not another status, decision or ledger. Build it from the same versioned operating-profile material in the issuance transaction, not by scanning JSON in the fleet. Index the local subject/scope lookup, asset key lookup and exact binding/qualification link. Link the enforced qualification version from the existing versioned WorkPattern binding scope, not generic `policyJson`. Validate typed scope on both read and write. Historical transitions stay in governed decision and receipt storage. Issuance and a typed effect receipt commit together with the same validated subject/version and evidence digest. Repeating the same authorized and still-compatible request returns the existing result, while changed inputs fail closed or require a new assessment and issue event. A rejected result is evidence in the adjudication stream, never an active assertion.

TAK-JSI business status supports active, pending-revalidation, restricted, suspended, expired and revoked. Reject and unaffected are impact/outcome projections, not extra states on an active record. Expiry applies on read at the earlier of declared expiry and required review deadline, even if a sweep is late. Restricted does not advertise as a current qualification or provide a positive S5/TAK ceiling. Any permitted supervised continuity must have separate existing authority and must not rely on the restricted record as permission. The GAID-qualified claim and Agent Card, where admitted, project the same record and status with minimum necessary evidence references. No second badge state, raw assessment output or reference that lets an unauthorized verifier read protected evidence. No new public attestation protocol in this slice.

## WorkPattern change interlock

1. **Freeze the transition.** Use the current WorkPattern candidate and activation/rollback lane. Resolve before and target manifests, calculate canonical change identity, and compare changed stable inputs and revisions. Start from the indexed dependency and binding associations. Include active records that intersect the candidate domain even if their impact turns out to be none. A record outside that candidate domain need not receive an irrelevant decision. An unknown shared dependency or incomplete reverse index stops the affected promotion.
2. **Record one attributable outcome per candidate.** The governed decision/effect receipt is keyed by change identity, qualification version and pre-change status revision. Bind actor, policy, old and target fingerprints, impacted dimensions, disposition, reason and next owner. Preserve an unaffected record only when explicit no-impact is recorded against this change and the record remains compatible with its existing scope. Mark affected records pending-revalidation, restricted or suspended as the scheme dictates. Rejection leaves the candidate inactive. No-impact never widens an old record to fit different material.
3. **Fence before the change can take effect.** Keep the existing serializable scope-keyed transaction in WorkPattern activation persistence. Share its transaction boundary with issuance/invalidation for overlapping subject/scope lanes. Writers that modify a shared input and affect multiple lanes acquire the affected keys in one stable order or publish a source revision fence before the changed input can be used, then hold activation while that revision's candidate set is resolved. Validation includes current input revisions and current candidate coverage to prevent a concurrent newly issued row or changed asset escaping impact. If a bounded batch cannot enumerate and settle the candidate set in one transaction, persist a held change in the existing Workroom transition and require a complete, immutable receipt set before activation. Retries re-read the exact source and never count partial coverage as complete.
4. **Assess target, then bind.** A passing target assessment may mint a new exact version while the candidate method is still inactive. Activation consumes the current impact receipts and rechecks the current subject/profile/version, qualification, binding/grants, intersected scope, data and regulatory policy, and experiment/operational evidence within the activation transaction. Persist the selected qualification reference and TAK decision receipt with the binding effect. A changed target, disappearing source, missing receipt or narrower grant blocks or narrows the action. A valid qualification alone does not activate a method.
5. **Recheck at use and rollback.** The binding reader and TAK effect path use the same resolver on current inputs and intersect the active binding with the qualification. The effect boundary revalidates against a revision or equivalent transaction fence. A stale launch projection is never an allow. Rollback is another transition with an impact decision and fresh compatibility checks for the actual fallback profile. It may restore a prior method only with independently valid current qualification and other authority. Otherwise hold the governed activity. Changes made outside WorkPattern promotion cannot keep an old positive result on fingerprint mismatch; a governing writer must route affected revalidation through the same change resolver before re-enabling qualified use.

The existing [WorkPattern read model](../../../apps/web/lib/tak/work-pattern-read-model.ts) supplies the Living Playbooks projection. Show which activity is affected, the outcome and what is owed next (assessment, scope reduction, reinstatement or operator decision). Keep record identity, evidence versions and blocked dimensions behind the existing evidence drill-down, subject to read authorization. Do not add an approval queue.

## S5 consumer contract

BI-687E7D81 can adopt this read contract before the issuer ships. S5 calls the shared producer behind `coordinator-eligibility.ts` with server-resolved admission context: exact subject/GAID, assigned job and activity, operating-profile revision, installation and scoped context. The producer owns applicability, evidence and compatibility. S5 separately resolves the explicit binding for the same subject and context, and intersects the returned JSI ceiling with current binding, job/grants, Workroom stage and runtime controls. Neither S5 nor the DPF2 launcher supplies or reinterprets a qualification fact. The same resolver serves launch and invocation, with a bounded batch entry for the Workroom drive.

| Typed outcome | Meaning and action |
| --- | --- |
| Not applicable | No scheme applies to this admitted activity, established by the server. No positive qualification claim or JSI-derived tools. Separately authorized no-scheme activity continues under its existing oversight. |
| Required-unavailable | Policy requires JSI but scheme, compatible record or serving support is unavailable, or required identity/profile is unresolved. Hold the qualified path. A separate no-scheme activity may be admitted only through its own policy. |
| Unknown | An authoritative source failed to read, was malformed, timed out or presented ambiguous current coverage. Never translate to not-applicable or eligible. Hold the qualified path and name the unavailable source. |
| Stale / incompatible / suspended / narrowed | Present evidence cannot authorize the requested job/activity/operation/context. Return a stable reason distinguishing the state. If a separately checked exact request is wholly inside an active record's narrower ceiling that request can qualify. Never treat a restricted **record status** as a positive outcome. |
| Eligible with ceiling | Return selected qualification ID/version and status revision, exact scheme/job/profile references, permitted operation keys, typed constraints, and earliest validity deadline. These are provenance and narrowing inputs, not a bearer credential. |

Before qualified rollout, S5 can exercise no-scheme routing and fail-closed required paths. A fixture can test composition deterministically, but it cannot prove issued fitness. It is legitimate for two exempt jobs to have different tools because other inputs differ. That does not meet S5's positive TAK-JSI exit. For that proof, hold unrelated gates sufficiently constant to isolate JSI, use two distinct approved GAIDs with real assessments, and show one operation available to one and withheld from the other because of JSI. Also require one admitted operation for each, and a live refusal after invalidation, without relying on a client hook. DPF2 receives only the resulting launch projection and credential as specified in S5.

## Fleet safety and verification

This proposed implementation touches deployment contracts [3, 4, 9 and 10](2026-05-09-deployment-contracts.md#the-canonical-deployment-contracts) for lifecycle, canonical identity, exact provider routing and the server consumer boundary. It adds no service, host default, identity API change or deployment-specific entitlement. The [data-model stewardship runbook](../../architecture/data-model-stewardship-runbook.md) and [fleet-safe evolution design](2026-07-03-fleet-safe-schema-evolution-design.md) own schema mechanics.

- **Expand.** Add empty qualification and dependency relations with indexed keys, new-table-only constraints, required `@dpf` lifecycle/sensitivity metadata and a field-level data classification review. Treat records as restricted evidence-bearing domain records and agree retention/lineage behavior with the steward before schema approval. No existing row is tightened, no positive credentials backfilled, no outside scheme enabled by schema presence. Use references or digests to evidence retained by its owner. If a later constraint touches populated data, use the fleet-safe remediation or validation sequence, not a replacement migration.
- **Converge before enabling.** Deploy compatible readers first, with a server-owned rollout fence. Do not enable qualified launch, issuance for use, or interlocked activation while an old writer/reader can consume an active binding without a live check. Existing bindings are neither presumed qualified nor mass-invalidated due to migration. Existing explicitly non-scheme work can continue, but no legacy compatibility path may run an activity configured as qualification-required. Fence or drain old executors before switching a scope. Missing, stale and unreadable are tested independently. Roll back by disabling qualified issue/admission, revoking affected sessions, and retaining rows and evidence. Never deploy an old checkless reader into a scope still relying on the new control. Contract only after fleet compatibility and gate evidence, without dropping history.
- **Verify.** Use a populated upgrade snapshot and clean install in governed nonprod. Verify owner-scoped queries and indexes at pilot limits, deterministic fingerprints, changed/missing/ambiguous policy, issuer/assessor separation, time boundaries, replay, revalidation race in Postgres, rollback race, and the same-source hook-free two-coworker S5 and WorkPattern canaries. Capture qualified and refused TAK receipts, no-effect proof for refusals, and Living Playbooks narrow/wide and light/dark evidence using existing components. The implementation plan must assign at least 20% of effort to converging profile material/diff and binding transition readers and writers.
- **Gate order.** Record artifact-bound research and canonical-design evidence, obtain independent spec approval and architecture/data/security review as applicable, and bind the implementation plan to the approved scope and live backlog coverage. Obtain independent plan review and authorized dependency dispositions before implementation. The design does not enable runtime behavior or close BI-6DB95601.

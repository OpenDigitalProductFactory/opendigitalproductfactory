Just now

    Overview first

    One square per room, grouped by the four portfolios. 300 rooms fit without scrolling. Rooms with no portfolio get their own group so the gap stays visible.

    Zoom and filter

    The five counts filter the map. A group name opens rows with a stage rail and the current action. The stage lens plots each room's age against the usual time for that step.

    Details on demand

    The side panel is the room's checklist: done, current, still to do. It shows the live action, the blocker and the next person. "Open room" goes to the existing detail page.

    Operations have a rhythm

    Standing rooms repeat; they do not advance through stages. They are drawn as their recent runs, so a missed cycle is as visible as a stuck project.

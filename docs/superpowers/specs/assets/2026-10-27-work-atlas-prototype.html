How this view is shaped

Just now

    Same squares everywhere

    State is never re-coded per business. A person who looks after a vet practice and an HVAC firm reads both without relearning, and shape keeps it readable without colour.

    Layout comes from the business

    Each archetype already maps to one of 13 operational layouts. The Atlas reuses that mapping: a floor and service day, a territory and technician board, an appointment book and a ward.

    Refinements change operations, not labels

    Takeout adds a lane timed against promises. Exotics adds enclosure checks. Large animal swaps the book for a farm round. Cuisine shows up as the kitchen's production pattern.

    Location shapes it too

    Zones are the business's own drawn service areas. The jurisdiction decides which standing checks appear. The season decides what the question asks first.

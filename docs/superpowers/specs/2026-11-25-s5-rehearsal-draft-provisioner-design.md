# Governed rehearsal draft for the S5 Contributor preview

Status: F1 amendment candidate for independent review. BI-1F994B73, WC-14E7E2F3. Builds on the approval producer merged in PR #7234 (BI-EA9E62F5), follows the parent BI-687E7D81, and applies only to the synthetic rehearsal policy on BI-588688A7. It neither relaxes v1 nor authorizes a rehearsal send.

## Decision

Use the existing Contributor preview and exact Workroom command boundary to persist one reviewed intent and its provenance receipt together. The issuer of the receipt is the governed Contributor preview owner, acting through its server-only provisioning command. Neither the fixture, requester, subject, Control, nor a generic state update can issue it. Approval creation and the Inbox decision both require the same original receipt and independently verified current authority. This avoids a second approval or transferable provenance service.

The admitted coworker creates the Workroom on behalf of a distinct synthetic human local to the isolated preview. The human is both the Workroom requester and TaskRun user. The real owner's approving Principal is neither of those actors. The same human performs the separate enrollment and custody duties described below. This is separation between the synthetic requester role and the approving Principal, not evidence that two different people acted. The ownership helper deliberately attributes coworker creation to its delegating human. Creating on behalf of the install owner therefore fails. No general ownership changes.

### Enrollment prerequisite

Use a canonical `User` and linked human `Principal` through the existing identity spine, including `syncUserPrincipal(userId, tx)` and its unique local `user` alias. Enroll a least-privilege, nonsuperuser account with a deterministic address under `s5-rehearsal.invalid` and record the reviewed fixture reference in the enrollment evidence. The reserved `.invalid` name is a label, never an authentication claim. Do not deliver mail, use real personal data, or set automation fields. The provisioner adds no migration and reads the `S5RehearsalRequesterEnrollment` record owned by BI-9F7192F9. [RFC 2606 §2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2606#section-2)

The owner ruled on 2026-11-25, **“I create and hold it.”** The owner creates this account once and authorises the coworker's delegation through the governed preview UI. All credential creation and custody stay inside that operator flow. No agent sees, holds, mints or reissues the credential. The provisioner independently verifies the resulting User, Principal, fixture evidence and current delegation. It never enrolls the user, copies a credential, creates or revokes a credential itself, or substitutes its own delegation. The admitted coworker opens the Workroom through the existing path on the verified synthetic user's behalf. The owner's real approving identity cannot be the requester or TaskRun user.

The generic `createUserAccount` action uses the default client, accepts a password and syncs the Principal outside the user write. It cannot establish preview isolation, exact replay and atomic attribution as it stands. BI-9F7192F9 owns server-side inactive enrollment and retirement. BI-9F5D1052 owns activation, the preview-only operator UI, delegation confirmation, sessions and issuance. The BI-9F7192F9 enrollment receipt records the setup key and the exact attempt reference allocated by the one-shot owner. The UI does not acquire the reservation. The read-only owner seams supply the receipt and current User, alias, delegation and revocation facts without bearer material. If that path cannot enforce the preview fence or the provisioner cannot prove the resulting records, hold without enrollment through a generic action or fixture. The custody ruling authorises no agent to exercise the UI.

## Objectives and acceptance

- OBJ-REHEARSAL-PROVENANCE — Only the active, exact Contributor lease, reviewed source and reviewed fixture on the isolated database can produce a draft for the exact Workroom, task, stage and attempt.
- OBJ-REHEARSAL-INDEPENDENCE — The requester and TaskRun user resolve to one verified synthetic human Principal and account, distinct from the installation owner's approving Principal and account. The same owner may act in both operator flows. This proves separation of roles, not two independent people. The coworker and Control receiver are verified separately.
- OBJ-REHEARSAL-NO-EFFECT — Provisioning does not approve, issue a permit or credential, start, wake or send. Replay cannot create a second intent or replenish the one-shot allowance.

## Grounding and research

The parent [Workroom-bound-toolset design](2026-11-25-workroom-bound-toolset-design.md) already owns the restricted activity, explicit subject and stage authority intersection, and server lifecycle. The approval producer merged in PR #7234 (BI-EA9E62F5) owns the exact shape and independent decision. The merged reader validates the draft bytes but has no governed provenance writer. The missing seam is not a retry or a code transport problem.

- GitHub's protected deployment rules separate who initiates from who approves and can restrict eligible environments and branches. Adopt enforceable separation and environment scope. A branch label is not sufficient proof of the running lease, SHA or DB. [GitHub environments](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/deployments-and-environments)
- Sigstore Cosign checks attestation identity and applies policy to the attested content. Adopt verifying issuer and scope, not just possession of a digest. Reject carrying a new signed document or key service across this same-database step. [Sigstore attestations](https://docs.sigstore.dev/cosign/verifying/attestation/)
- SLSA v1.2 verifies artifact identity and provenance against expectations from outside the artifact, including trusted builder identity and expected inputs. Apply that pattern to the reviewed fixture digest and independent lease, source, requester and attempt facts, without claiming this record is a SLSA attestation. [SLSA verification](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.2/verifying-artifacts)

Reuse `apps/web/lib/nonprod/environment-lease-provenance.ts` and `preview-runtime-fence.ts` as evidence for ownership and exact runtime identity. The fence's cleanup effects are not part of this flow. Use the owning lease adapter and authoritative preview execution proof. A development environment variable, host name, plausible fixture reference, or HTTP Origin on its own cannot prove them. Use `apps/web/lib/work-capsules/command-receipts.ts` for command/Workroom locking and the atomic receipt. `apps/web/lib/work-capsules/oauth-workroom-ownership.ts` and `apps/web/lib/work-management/workroom-agent-access.server.ts` define the creator/requester split and the human/coworker intersection. The approval producer merged in PR #7234 (BI-EA9E62F5) supplies the strict draft parser and the proposal and decision sites to fence. Use existing authenticated Control evidence and the S5 coordinator eligibility seam. Do not invent a parallel identity, lease, JSI, or Control verifier.

## Invoker and positive non-production fence

The invoker is the admitted coworker identified by `TaskRun.progressPayload.nonprodPreviewOwner.agentId` on the exact active lease's canonical owner TaskRun. Resolve its active local agent Principal and require the authenticated coworker and human delegation to match that record's `agentId`, `userId` and the lease owner session through `ownsProcessFreeLease`. Do not accept a caller-supplied owner identifier. The authenticated delegating human Principal may be the install owner's approving Principal. The synthetic requester Principal and account must remain distinct from that approving Principal and account. The invoking coworker Principal is distinct from the S5 subject and cannot attest its own subject, requester or Control pins. This is role separation, not a requirement for another human. The server-only Contributor preview orchestration command uses the existing `work_capsule_write` intersection, the exact lease-owner checks and the one-shot reservation. Any missing or mismatched ownership record holds. No agent or coworker gets `manage_users` or any credential-issuing grant. Only the owner operates the UI under BI-9F5D1052.

1. **Registration.** In the preview owner loader, require positive non-production attestation of this installation identity. Reuse the pinned organization material and `verifyMembershipChain` in the federation membership owner, including the non-production class in the leaf, validity and binding to the exact local installation. Unattested, production, expired or mismatched identity means no registration. The certificate parser alone cannot prove that the presented identity owns this preview. `DPF_ENVIRONMENT_CLASS`, URL host, stored lease row and default class resolution cannot substitute. A preview with no governed attestation-to-lease join is held on BI-8EA98961.
2. **Before effects.** On the same connection/transaction used for writes, positively verify the actual PostgreSQL endpoint and database identity against an independent, current lease-owner attestation for the dedicated isolated DB. Reuse the connection readback pattern in `packages/db/src/sanitized-clone.ts` (name, address and port), but a copied row, DNS label, database name or those values alone cannot prove uniqueness across container restarts. Require the owner-proven server or cluster identity and exact database. BI-5C193A3F owns the least-privilege verified connection binding; missing proof holds. Recheck the active lease, attested installation, source and DB before proposing, deciding and minting. No default client fallback or attempt to repair verification inputs.
3. **Enrollment uses the same fence.** The owner-operated enrollment UI cannot create the user, sync the human Principal or issue a credential on a different connection or target. The provisioner verifies the resulting enrollment and delegation before making any Workroom write. Test an otherwise-valid lease served by a production or unattested install, and a non-production process pointed at the wrong DB. Both refuse before any write.

## Idempotency, rollback and non-goals

The approval ruling is the global one-shot key. The setup key is the one-shot owner's exact reservation identity, the pair `{ rulingRef, reservationId }`. The owner-operated UI enrolls at most one exact synthetic requester under its own existing authority and records the setup key and reserved attempt reference in the enrollment receipt. It does not take the one-shot reservation. Before any Workroom write, the provisioner independently confirms the active reservation and matches both parts of the setup key, the exact attempt ref and the reviewed fixture to that enrollment receipt. Missing or mismatched refs hold without a Workroom or TaskRun write. Exact replay uses the same enrollment record, unique normalized `User.email` and local `user` alias. It never creates or changes the User, Principal, delegation or credential. The owner UI's enrollment uniqueness prevents a second account for the same setup, independently of the Workroom fence. An email without the exact enrollment receipt does not get adopted. Workroom creation uses its governed unique `idempotencyKey` and canonical TaskRun linkage. Recovery re-observes the same identities and locked attempt rather than creating another. Once approved or spent, no second rehearsal may be provisioned across rooms, DB restores or new leases. Only a new owner ruling may authorize another.

The draft and provenance receipt commit atomically. The owner-operated enrollment transaction rolls back its User and Principal if the alias or receipt cannot be persisted. Enrollment and delegation remain under operator custody and are tracked with the exact setup key. Hold all use until the joined setup is verified. On failure, the agent quarantines and holds the attempt, closes any partial Workroom, cancels the TaskRun, releases the exact lease and files the operator request. Only the owner retires the synthetic identity and revokes its credential, delegation and sessions through the governed preview UI. The provisioner verifies the owner's exact-attempt revocation and retirement evidence without accessing bearer material or calling a user or credential mutator. BI-9F7192F9 owns server-side retirement of the inactive or quarantined account. BI-9F5D1052 owns the governed operator cleanup path and revocation of credentials, delegation and sessions. Read back each action before calling rollback complete. Rollback never refunds an approved or spent allowance or deletes its evidence. An unconfirmed credential cleanup keeps the setup quarantined. If the separate steps lack a durable compensation receipt, do not register the setup.

No schema migration, generic role or Inbox changes, alternate identity/approval service, live activation, or provider call. Provisioning never approves, issues the S5 stage permit or S5 room credential, posts, wakes, or sends. Production, v1, API fallback and real data stay out of scope.

Connect business mail

DPF will bring permitted business messages into owned work and follow up when nobody acknowledges them. Replies wait for approval.

1. Choose the mailbox

Other providers retain their existing advanced connection form.

2. Choose the business messages

For a mixed personal and business mailbox, start with specific business correspondents. No Gmail folders or labels are needed.

Your own mailbox address cannot be used as the filter: that would include almost all your mail. DPF can suggest known business contacts when your permissions allow it.

What DPF will read

Messages to or from these correspondents, starting with the last 30 days. DPF checks about once an hour. Changing this selection requires another review before importing more mail.

3. Review and connect securely

Google grants account-wide read access. DPF will request only messages matching your approved business selection. The selection is enforced by DPF; it is not a separate Google permission.

No send, delete or mailbox-edit permission is requested for this connection. Draft replies stay in DPF for approval.

Google connection setup is needed. The platform setup owner configures the Google application on DPF's secure settings page. Do not paste a password or secret into chat.

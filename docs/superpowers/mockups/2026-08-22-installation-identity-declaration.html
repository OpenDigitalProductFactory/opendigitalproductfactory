Rules this prototype encodes

  1. The authority split holds. The installer owns the host environment fact. The portal writes semantic intent to installation.operating-intent.v1 and a lower-ranked environment projection to installation.environment-class.v1. The portal never writes installer state.
  2. Precedence is shown, not hidden. Process override, then installer state, then the portal declaration, then the default. When a higher tier wins, the panel says so and names the flag that would change it.
  3. Undeclared means production. The cautious default is stated in plain words, never as a blank field.
  4. A material change needs a preview. A changed environment, job, or pairing shows the field diff, the stance diff, and the stale evidence before anything is written.
  5. No silent re-confirm. A material change saved without the preview is refused. Stored confirmation.status reaches confirmed only when the operator confirms the preview they saw.
  6. A stance is a brake. The panel never presents a stance as a grant, and the read view says so.
  7. Detail is deferred. The read view is a lead sentence plus four rationales. The form and the impact tables live behind one disclosure.

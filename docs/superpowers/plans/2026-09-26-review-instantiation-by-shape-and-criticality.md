## Risks and recovery

Membership/task races, revoked authority, stale marking/head and partial
checkpoint effects are the primary failure paths. Keep admission transactional
and every inference current. Disable new admission when unsafe without deleting
existing requests or completed evidence. Unknown effects remain parked.

Reverting source is not proof that pending holds are safely released. Preserve
task/decision/room evidence and reconcile pending work through its supported
state machine; do not replay paid work, erase findings or label pending work
complete. Scope a rollback to the qualified behavior and record any remaining
workflow reconciliation.

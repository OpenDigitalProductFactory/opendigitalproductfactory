## Risks and rollback

The main risks are cross-customer disclosure through thread lookup, duplicate effects during concurrent intake, clocks falsely satisfied by drafts, unowned work hidden by a holding reply, and stale result/authority reused after recovery. Negative customer/mailbox and real-transaction tests are release prerequisites. Missing related authority or current-consent infrastructure is an explicit dependency, never a reason to create a parallel permission or recovery path.

Rollback uses a scoped revert and the normal protected release route. Preserve all received mail, tickets, comments, drafts and clock evidence; do not delete business records or reverse committed migrations. The existing correspondence queue remains the safe visible fallback when service association cannot proceed. Any schema extension must be additive and forward-safe so earlier code can retain records it does not yet understand. Reconcile pending jobs and uncertain effects through their original owners before re-enabling service intake.

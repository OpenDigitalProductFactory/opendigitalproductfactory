Risks and rollback

Refactor the hierarchy without losing the record

  • Risk: splitting the public overview creates dead links. Control: move by stable section anchors, keep redirects where necessary, and run the link checker.
  • Risk: visual polish hides stale claims. Control: verify claims before layout work and make maturity states explicit.
  • Risk: screenshots become stale or expose private data. Control: prefer diagrams; use curated fixtures and sanitized captures when screenshots are uniquely useful.
  • Risk: parallel guide work conflicts. Control: respect capsule scope; the active docs/user-guide/ai-workforce/* claim is excluded from this branch.
  • Rollback: each surface ships in its own PR. Reverting one slice restores the prior source without affecting the other two or the docs-system substrate.

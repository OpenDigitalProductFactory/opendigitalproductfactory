What this category has in common

Restaurants book tables; caterers quote events and invoice after delivery; bakeries take orders and custom-cake commissions. Three shapes, one storefront engine. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Tables get double-booked. Table × duration is blocked so reservations don’t collide.
  • !Dietary needs land as free text and vanish. Allergens and dietary requirements are captured up front for the kitchen.
  • !Catering quote → invoice is disconnected. An inquiry-to-quote flow keeps the event together from proposal to invoice.
  • !Bakeries can’t mix shop + custom cake. A custom-order sub-flow routes commissions to the baker inside the order.

What the category prepares for

  • ✓Event catering flow from inquiry to quote to post-event invoice. Invoicing & finance
  • ✓Bakery storefront with cart checkout plus a custom-cake inquiry sub-flow. Website & enquiries
  • ✓Allergen capture as a duty-of-care field. AI coworker & workflow
  • ✓Per-shape billing — per visit, post-event, per order. Invoicing & finance

Shared concerns — not a universal workflow

What this category has in common

Scheduled mobile service (glass, mechanic, detailing, tyre) is booked and paid at completion; roadside and lockout work is real-time emergency dispatch with no pre-booking. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Glass quotes need VIN-to-part matching. The VIN is captured up front so parts resolution and pricing aren’t a guessing game on the phone.
  • !Mechanics dispatch blind. Vehicle year/make/model and the symptom ride along with the job.
  • !Roadside isn’t an “appointment”. A real-time, transactional dispatch mode handles lockouts and tows without forcing them into a booking calendar.
  • !Post-install compliance goes undocumented. Auto-glass ADAS recalibration is tracked as a step, not an afterthought.

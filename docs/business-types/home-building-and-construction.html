Problems often seen here

  • !Inquiries don’t qualify the buyer. Bedroom count, budget, buying status, and timeline are captured so the quote is educated.
  • !There’s no clean way to book a design consult. A consultation booking item slots a 60-minute, business-hours meeting.
  • !Design selections are lost. The projects module ties selections to the customer record.
  • !Model-home photos are the storefront. Required facility galleries keep the hero and model sections alive.

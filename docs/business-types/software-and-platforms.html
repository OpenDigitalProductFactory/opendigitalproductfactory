Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Prospects look like customers. Vocabulary distinguishes users, developers, and enterprise prospects from end-customers.
  • !Inquiry → backlog is manual. A “send to product backlog” action turns a lead into a tracked item on the product.
  • !Pilots and partnerships are invisible. Service agreements track pilot terms, feature requests, and co-sell outcomes.
  • !Platform-speak leaks to customers. Internal product language stays separate from customer-facing copy.

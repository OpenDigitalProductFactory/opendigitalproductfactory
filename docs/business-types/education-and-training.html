What this category has in common

One-to-one lessons are slot-based and instructor-assigned; corporate training runs inquiry → proposal → program enrolment. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Learner and payer are different people. A parent books; the child attends. Both are captured so the instructor knows who’s who.
  • !Term packages are invisible. Lifecycle signals flag “package depleted” and “term ends”.
  • !Driving lessons need instructor & vehicle. The booking captures both.
  • !Corporate follow-up is forgotten. The B2B inquiry-to-proposal flow stores context for the next program.

Profile patterns

What the category prepares for

  • ✓Learner-vs-payer intake for parents, students, employees, and participants. Website & enquiries
  • ✓Instructor & location assignment with term-based and drop-in patterns. AI coworker & workflow
  • ✓Accurate durations for 30-minute piano, 60-minute driving, 90-minute workshops. AI coworker & workflow
  • ✓B2B training framing — delegates, programs, L&D outcomes. AI coworker
  • ✓Package & term lifecycle signalling renewal. Reminders & renewals
Trust & compliance. Safeguarding for minors (parent/guardian consent capture) and instructor qualifications are handled at the capability layer.

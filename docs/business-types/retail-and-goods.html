Shared concerns — not a universal workflow

What this category has in common

Browse the catalogue, add to cart, check out, get an order reference. Wholesale runs as a B2B trade-pricing inquiry. Repeat buyers should be recognised, not treated as one-time. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Broken or missing product images lose the sale. Required gallery slots catch missing images before a customer bounces.
  • !Custom commissions get lost in checkout. An inquiry sub-flow lets made-to-order and buy-now coexist in one storefront.
  • !Florists need a delivery date, not just billing. The order captures the recipient address and delivery date for perishables.
  • !Wholesale buyers can’t see trade pricing. A separate “request trade pricing” path serves B2B accounts.

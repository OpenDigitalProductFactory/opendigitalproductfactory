What this category has in common

Account-based fees behind a KYC gate. DPF is the engagement layer — it records applications and obligations and never moves money; core banking stays with the institution. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Disclosures must come before the CTA. FDIC, NCUA, and NMLS/RESPA/TILA disclosures gate the inquiry.
  • !Credit unions are member-owned. A membership-eligibility check and member-owner vocabulary replace “customer”.
  • !Loan inquiries lack scenario context. Purchase, refinance, or HELOC is captured up front.
  • !Rate quotes are buried. An interactive calculator fed by the form estimates the monthly payment.

Profile patterns

What the category prepares for

  • ✓Mandatory disclosures before any inquiry can proceed. Licence & safety checks
  • ✓A loan / rate calculator with APR and rate disclaimers. Website & enquiries
  • ✓KYC-gated provisioning — DPF records applications at the engagement layer only. Licence & safety checks
  • ✓Prepared-not-prescribed billing — fee obligations recorded, money never moved. Invoicing & finance
  • ✓BIAN-grounded vocabulary — borrowers, members, accounts, loans. AI coworker
Trust & compliance. The strictest profile: FDIC (banks), NCUA (credit unions), and NMLS/RESPA/TILA (mortgage). KYC/AML gates intake and rate/fee disclaimers are mandatory.

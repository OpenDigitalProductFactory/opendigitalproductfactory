The animal journey

One journey, three accountable value streams

An animal enters through intake, remains in health and welfare care until it is ready, then moves through adoption and placement. A failed placement returns safely to intake and care; it does not disappear from the record.

Primary value stream 1

Intake and safe placement

Bring a stray, surrendered, or transferred animal into the rescue safely and place it within real capacity.

Starts with

Stray report, surrender request, or partner handoff

Finishes with

Identified and triaged animal in an appropriate kennel or foster placement

Primary value stream 3

Adoption and placement

Match an adoption-ready animal to a suitable home, transfer custody safely, and support or reverse the placement when needed.

Starts with

Animal approved for adoption or specialist placement

Finishes with

Stable placement, or an evidenced return and re-entry into care

Shared concerns — not a universal workflow

What this category has in common

Money comes from gifts, dues, or member-owned shares. A gift gets a receipt, not a bill — and there is no account behind it. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Gift forms wrongly make a bill. DPF sends a receipt and skips the account — your coworker knows there is no customer here.
  • !Co-ops can’t track member shares. Member-owned tools split the profit back to members fairly.
  • !Shared gear is first-come, first-served. Members book it, use it, and bring it back — with fair turns for all.
  • !Rescues have no list of pets to adopt. A photo page shows which pets are ready for a home.

Profile patterns

What the category prepares for

  • ✓Take gifts — one-off, monthly, or private — with a receipt every time, never a bill. AI coworker & workflow
  • ✓Member-owned tools to track who can join, what they own, and how profit is shared. AI coworker & workflow
  • ✓Shared-gear booking for co-op machines, with fair turns for members. Website & enquiries
  • ✓Volunteer route planning for meal-delivery rounds. Work board
  • ✓The right words — supporters, donors, members — never “customers” or “bills”. AI coworker & workflow
Trust & compliance. 501(c)(3) framing, gift-aid / tax-relief language, and cooperative member-democratic governance (patronage allocation) are built in.

Shared concerns — not a universal workflow

What this category has in common

Slot-based grooming, multi-night boarding, and recurring walks — all anchored to the pet’s record so service arrives prepared. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !Groomers don’t know the dog until it arrives. Size, coat, and temperament are captured so they arrive prepped.
  • !Boarding can’t block date ranges cleanly. A date-range picker reserves the kennel Fri–Mon without conflicts.
  • !Walk frequency is invisible. Daily or 3×/week schedules surface the next walk automatically.
  • !Medical alerts go missing. Allergies and medication ride along on the pet record.

Profile patterns

What the category prepares for

  • ✓A pet record for name, size, breed, temperament, alerts, and vaccination status. Customer records
  • ✓Three booking shapes — slots (grooming), date ranges (boarding), recurring (walking). Reminders & renewals
  • ✓Size-based pricing rendered from the catalogue. Website & enquiries
  • ✓Recall signals — “Fluffy is due for a nail trim.” Reminders & renewals
  • ✓Travel buffers for mobile grooming and house-call vets. AI coworker & workflow
Trust & compliance. Vaccination-status checks at grooming intake and rabies/controlled-substance handling for mobile vets sit at the capability layer.

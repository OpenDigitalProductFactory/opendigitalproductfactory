What this category has in common

Members join and pay a recurring membership for access to facilities and classes. The renewal — not the first sale — is the business. The exact operating flow belongs to each organization type above.

Common pressure points

Problems often seen here

  • !The coworker treats it like a one-off sale. Subscriptions and renewals are first-class — never a one-time “appointment”.
  • !Class schedules are hard to model. Recurring class patterns (Mon 6pm Hatha, Wed 6pm Vinyasa) slot in cleanly.
  • !Tiers disappear in the chaos. Gold/Silver/Basic render with recurring-price language up front.
  • !Liability data isn’t collected. Age and emergency contact are captured at signup.
